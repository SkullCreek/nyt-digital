## Title

100 AI Video Prompts for Interior Design Reels

## Headline

Turn your finished rooms into video ads that book clients.

## Description

Your best work lives in still photos, and nobody stops scrolling for a photo.
This kit turns the project photos you already have into 20-second video ads, shot by shot, with no videographer and no filming.

Most AI room videos look fake, or quietly change the room: a bigger window, an extra lamp, a door that was never there.
Every prompt in this kit ends with a lock line that keeps your walls, windows and furniture exactly as you designed them.
Only the camera, the light and small details move.

You get 10 complete ad stories, each told in 10 shots.
Pick the one that fits the project you have, paste the prompts into Seedance 2.5 one shot at a time, then follow the CapCut edit.

## Frequently asked questions

**What exactly do I get?**
An instant download with three parts: the 120-page guide (PDF) with all 100 video prompts and the image prompts for your start frames, the Prompt Library (Word document that opens in Google Docs, plus text files) for clean copy and paste, and 16 printable worksheets (PDF).

**Do I need video editing experience?**
No. There is no filming at all: the videos are made from your photos. If you can copy, paste and trim a clip on your phone, you can make every ad. The CapCut steps are written out for each one.

**Which AI tools do I need, and are they included?**
You need an image tool (Nano Banana in Gemini, ChatGPT or Midjourney), Seedance 2.5 for video (on Higgsfield or another host) and CapCut, which is free. The tools are not included and are paid separately. Plan on a few dollars of AI credits per ad. The prompts are written and tested for Seedance 2.5 only.

**Can I use these for any design style?**
Yes. The prompts animate your own room photos, so they work with whatever you designed: modern, classic, minimalist or anything else. They move the camera and the light. They never restyle the room.

**How long does one ad take?**
Plan an afternoon for your first ad. Once you have made a few, about an hour per ad.

**When do I get access?**
Instantly after purchase. The files are yours to keep.

**What if I don't see results?**
These prompts are frameworks, not guarantees. AI models can produce unexpected results and may need a few tries per shot, and bookings depend on your work, your consistency and your audience.

**Can I get a refund?**
No. This is an instant digital download and results depend on third-party AI models, so all sales are final. If your files don't arrive or won't open, email info@nyt-studios.com and we'll send them again.
