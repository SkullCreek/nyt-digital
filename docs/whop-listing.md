## Full description

Your best work lives in still photos.
This kit turns them into video ads, shot by shot, without faking a single wall.

Every prompt includes a lock line that keeps your walls, windows and furniture exactly as you designed them.
Only the camera, the light and small details move.

**What you get (instant download, ZIP):**

- The guide (PDF, 120 pages): 10 ad types, each told in 10 shots, with image prompts for your start frames, CapCut edit steps, captions and 3 hooks per ad, a fix-it page, a 30-day plan and a glossary.
- Prompt Library (Word, opens in Google Docs, plus plain-text files): every prompt in its own box, placeholders highlighted, and which images to upload for each shot.
- Worksheets (printable PDF, 16 sheets): Room Brief, Permissions Check, a shot sheet for every ad, Clip Check Grid and Results Tracker.

**The 10 ads:** The Swipe (before/after), Come In (walkthrough), 6 AM to 11 PM (a day of light), Touch Test (materials), From Pin to Room (mood board), Lines to Light (sketch to room), Which One? (A or B), After Dark (lighting plan), It Fits (small space), Your Room, Next (the paid ad).

**Time:** plan an afternoon for your first ad, and about an hour per ad once you've made a few.

**You'll also need (not included, paid separately):** an image tool (Nano Banana, ChatGPT or Midjourney), Seedance 2.5 (on Higgsfield or another host) and CapCut (free).
Expect a few dollars of AI credits per ad.
Prompts are written and tested for Seedance 2.5 only.

**Refunds:** this is an instant digital download and results depend on third-party AI models, so all sales are final.
If your files don't arrive or won't open, email info@nyt-studios.com and we'll send them again.
